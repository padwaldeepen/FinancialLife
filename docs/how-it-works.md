# How This App Works — In Plain Words

> For anyone who opens this app for the first time — no technical or finance knowledge
> needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.
> (Features marked **[coming soon]** are planned and described in `plan.md`.)

---

## What is this?

A private money app that lives on **our own computer** — not on the internet, not owned
by a company. It watches where your money goes and helps you make better decisions with
it. Think of it as a personal money assistant that never sells your data, never charges
a subscription, and never shows ads — because we built it ourselves.

## Why does it exist?

Most people track money in one of two ways:

1. **A spreadsheet (Excel)** — you type everything by hand, and it never *tells* you
   anything. It just stores numbers and waits for you to figure them out.
2. **A finance app like Rocket Money** — smart, but you pay monthly for it, and your
   bank details and spending history live on a company's servers.

This app takes the best of both: the intelligence of the paid apps, with the privacy of
your own computer, at a cost of zero.

## How does it make life easier?

The app is built around three simple moments:

### 1. Getting money into the app takes seconds
- **Type it like a text message**: write `coffee 4.50` and it becomes a proper record —
  amount, category, date — automatically. No forms.
- **Snap a photo of a bill or receipt** **[coming soon]**: the app reads it, figures out
  the store and the amount, and files it under the right category (groceries, travel,
  and so on). If you accidentally add the same bill twice, it notices and asks.
- **Import a bank file**: download a file from your bank's website, drop it in, done.

### 2. One glance tells you if you're okay
Open the app and the first thing you see is one number: **safe to spend**
**[coming soon]** — how much you can spend before your next paycheck *after* rent,
bills, and everything already promised is set aside. No math, no guessing.

### 3. It notices things you'd miss
- "You're paying $15.99 every month to a service — did you know?" **[coming soon]**
- "Your food spending is 40% higher than usual this month." **[coming soon]**
- "Three bills are due in the next week, totaling $310."
- "At this pace you'll hit your savings goal in March." 

That's the difference from a spreadsheet: the spreadsheet waits for questions;
this app volunteers the answers.

## What do the screens show?

- **Home** — am I okay right now? The safe-to-spend number, what's due soon, what
  happened recently.
- **Activity** — everything you've spent and earned, searchable.
- **Recurring** — every bill and subscription that repeats, with what it costs per month
  in total. **[coming soon]**
- **Insights** — where the money goes: by month, by year, by category, by store, and how
  it's changing over time. **[coming soon]**
- **Manage** — the settings drawer: accounts, categories, and housekeeping.

On a **phone**, the app is deliberately simpler: check your number, snap a receipt, add
an expense. The deep analysis lives on the **computer**, where there's room for it.

## Who can use it?

Anyone in the family — each person gets **their own login and their own private money
view**. Nobody can see anyone else's finances, including the administrator.

When you sign up, you pick your country — **USA, India, or Canada** — and everything you
see is in that country's money (dollars or rupees, formatted the way you're used to).
If your life spans two countries, you can add a second country to your login: each
country is its own **separate world** with its own accounts, receipts, and dashboard —
you switch between them with one tap, and they are never mixed together.
**[country profiles coming with the rebuild]**

## Where does my data live? Is it safe?

- Everything stays **on this computer**. Nothing is uploaded to any company.
- The app never connects to your bank — *you* bring the data (type it, photograph it,
  or import a file), so there are no bank passwords stored anywhere.
- AI features run **on this computer too**. Sending anything to an online AI service
  only happens if it's explicitly switched on in settings — and it's off by default.
- Regular automatic backup copies protect against a broken computer. **[being set up]**

## What this app deliberately does NOT do

- ❌ No tax filing — taxes are handled elsewhere.
- ❌ No bank account linking — by design, for privacy.
- ❌ No subscriptions, ads, upsells, or "premium tiers" — there's nothing to buy.
- ❌ No reading your email or SMS.

## The goal, in one sentence

**Open the app once a day for ten seconds, know exactly where you stand, and once a week
let it show you one way to keep more of your own money.**
